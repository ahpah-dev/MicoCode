Set up your Windows app in three small steps +
  1. Download and open MicoCode. The installer works in your user profile; the portable app needs no installation.
  2. Open Models → Your routers. Use the same 9Router where you connected your providers. For the managed instance, choose Open 9Router dashboard to connect providers, then refresh the model catalogue. To connect a separate instance, choose Use existing and enter its API key.
  3. Select a model or combo from the full catalogue and start building. Model pricing and limits follow your connected providers. Local Ollama routes remain available for inference on your own computer.

A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you
make a little magic.

Is it really free? +

MicoCode is free to use. Models running on your computer or browser have no service quota; memory, context length, and speed depend on your hardware. Optional cloud models follow their provider's free-tier limits. We don't promise unlimited cloud inference.

Where does my work live? +

Your projects, conversations, and checkpoints stay on your device, in the Windows app’s private profile or your browser’s storage. Export a ZIP to keep a backup: clearing site data deletes the workspace. Local inference stays on your machine. Cloud mode sends your prompt and requested files to your chosen connection and its provider. Each router key is kept separately for this tab’s session.

What can I build? +

The browser edition works with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript websites. It includes an editor, file tools, AI plans and edits, live preview, basic project checks, checkpoints, and import/export. npm builds, an OS shell, Git remotes, backend servers, and deployment of generated projects require your local development tools.

Do I need to download anything? +

The Windows app includes its runtime and workspace. One click downloads and configures supported routers in a private folder. Ollama setup may download a large runtime and a coding model. Browser AI downloads a model once and needs a compatible GPU. OpenRouter and additional upstream accounts require your own key or sign-in. Anonymous AI Horde routes need no account; volunteer queues and availability vary.

Is this connected to OpenCode or Antigravity? +

MicoCode is an independent project inspired by agentic coding workflows. It is not affiliated with OpenCode, Google, or Antigravity.
